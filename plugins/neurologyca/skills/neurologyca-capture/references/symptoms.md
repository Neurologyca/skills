# Symptoms

## Contents

- [On-device](#on-device)
- [Your backend and the proxy](#your-backend-and-the-proxy)
- [Deferred (server-side) · Preview](#deferred-server-side--preview)
- [Transcription](#transcription)

Each row is checked against the installed SDK and https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/errors.md. Branch on the code, never on `message`: errors arrive by `onError` or are thrown, warnings arrive by `onWarning` and never stop the capture. A `recoverable` other than `true` during the steps means `abort()`; after `stop()`, never abort: Engine already has the capture.

## On-device

| Symptom | Code | Cause | Fix | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Building the client throws `taxonomy.contextId is required` | none | The configuration lacks the context | Copy `neuro.config.json` from the kit; Measurement uses `taxonomy: { contextId: 'measurement' }` | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/guides/measurement.md |
| `defineTenantConfig` throws that a field "must never live in this file" | none | A credential in `neuro.config.json` | Move it to the backend's `.env` | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/backend.md |
| `captureProtocol` throws before opening the camera | `E_TAXONOMY_INVALID` | A `situationId` without its `hciId` | A `situationId` needs its `hciId`: take both from the kit, or neither | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/errors.md |
| The camera does not open | `insecure-context` (a `MediaProblem`) | The page is not in a secure context | Serve it over HTTPS or from `localhost` | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/on-device.md |
| An option is refused before the camera opens | `E_UNKNOWN_OPTION` | An option this SDK version does not know; `createProtocolClient` refuses one too (also inside `startup`) | Do not invent options: check the signature; `signal` and `captureId` go in `captureProtocol` | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/reference.md |
| The startup screen shows nothing for minutes | `startup.stalled` | A download of the face model or of speech brought no new byte for `stallMs` (20 s by default); it cuts nothing | Paint `onStartupStage` messages; offer wait, Cancel (`signal`, which rejects with `E_CAPTURE_ABORTED`) or, for speech, `transcribe: false` on the same client | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/on-device.md |
| `captureProtocol` rejects before the first step | `E_CAPTURE` | `CaptureStartError` with `stage` and `reason`; `face-model` with `timeout` means the vision cap (120 s) ran out | Show `userMessage`; after `subStage: 'vision-runtime'` offer a reload, not a retry | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/errors.md |
| `captureProtocol` rejects with `reason: 'integrity'` | `E_CAPTURE` | `stage: 'face-model'`: the downloaded face model failed kodec's integrity check (a corrupted download or a network that alters it); the SDK does not retry it without the check | Show `userMessage` (`face-model.integrity`); offer a reload or another network, never a loop of retries | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/errors.md |
| Speech never arrives and the capture goes on, with `reason: 'integrity'` | `stt` with `subStage: 'stt-weights'` | A speech model file failed kodec's integrity check; `SessionReport.transcriptionLoad.reason` is `'integrity'`. Not every model is checked (never the voice-activity one; not the speech one in Safari and iOS or with `selfHost.modelHost`) | No transcript for that capture; do not loop. A reload or another network downloads it again | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/guides/load-models.md |
| Speech never arrives and the capture goes on | `stt` with `reason: 'timeout'` | The speech cap (600 s) ran out on a slow network | Expected: no transcript for that capture. Prepare speech earlier, or offer the deferred capture | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/guides/timeouts.md |
| `prepare()` resolved `ready` but Start downloads everything again | none (stages with `source: 'network'`) | The capture ran on another client: only the same client adopts | One client per page, prepare and capture with it, `client.release()` when leaving | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/on-device.md |
| The startup is slow: the person waits between Start and the first frame | none (`report.startup.prepared` is `null`, or its `face` is not `'adopted'`) | Nothing was prepared, it was prepared on another client, or every session loads the models again | Pick one of the five ways: B on the screen before Start, C for speech after consent, E with `keepPrepared` for several sessions (one `prepare()`, none between sessions). Compare `startup.totalMs` of your own first and second sessions | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/guides/load-models.md |
| The capture stutters: few frames per second or gaps on some devices | none: read the `'capture-quality'` events of `onCodecEvent`; `stt.mainThread` if speech runs on the main thread | The device: face detection on the GPU (WebGL 2), heat in long sessions on phones, on-device transcription, or hands and body turned on | Describe the session rate (without the first two windows and the hidden ones: the median, the windows below 8 fps and the lowest), never grade it; keep the tab visible; turn off what you do not need; where it stays low, offer deferred (Preview, only if the organization has it turned on) with the person's permission | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/start/devices-and-modes.md |
| No speech on a phone: the capture goes on without a transcript | `stt` with `blocker: 'no-webgpu'` (one of `no-webgpu`, `ladder-exhausted`, `video-cost-veto`, `no-memory-to-learn`, `dtype-required`) | Without WebGPU on-device transcription is refused unless the client allows `transcribe.allowWasmWorker` | Ask for speech before measuring: without a WebGPU adapter, `prepare({ models: { speech: true } })` gives `webgpu-adapter` `fail` and downloads no speech (the check with no model cannot tell); allow `transcribe.allowWasmWorker` (slower), or offer to record (deferred, Preview, if the organization has it on), with the person's permission: it transcribes on the server | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/start/devices-and-modes.md |
| On Safari or iOS, speech is not ready ahead and only the face is kept between sessions | none (`transcription: 'not-prepared'`, `partialReason: 'speech-not-prepared'`) | Safari and iOS prepare and keep only the face; speech runs on WASM and loads when the capture starts | Not a failure: prepare the face only and warn about the wait with `estimatedSpeechMs` (it tends to come out high) | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/guides/load-models.md |
| You see `prepare.face-lost` | none (a warning) | The prepared face rejected its first frames or lost its GPU context; kodec loads it again once. The report keeps the face `adopted` | Nothing to fix; if the reload fails the start fails with `E_CAPTURE` (`stage: 'first-frame'`). Call `client.release()` and prepare again | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/errors.md |
| `prepare()` rejects | `E_PREPARE` | `throwOnFailure: true` with `failed` or `aborted`, or `reason: 'capture-in-progress'` while a capture or baseline of the client runs | Prepare before, or once it ends | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/errors.md |
| The capture ends without results | `E_RESULTS_TIMEOUT` | Nothing within `outcomeDeadlineMs` (240 s by default); Engine is still processing | Do not recapture: read them later with `retrieveResults` and the pair | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/results-backend.md |
| `onError` with `recoverable: false` after `stop()` | `E_INTERPRETATION_TIMEOUT` | Indicators arrived, the interpretation did not: Engine's notice did not arrive and `/analyze` did not give it | Never `abort()` here: show the indicators and ask for the interpretation later from your backend | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/errors.md |
| `onError` with `recoverable: true` and status `timeout` after `stop()` | `E_ANALYSIS_IN_PROGRESS` | `/analyze` answered `202 ANALYSIS_IN_PROGRESS` and Engine was still writing the interpretation when the wait ran out | Show the results (they were delivered), say «interpreting» and read it later with `client.analyze()`, checking `inProgress` and waiting `retryAfterMs` | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/errors.md |
| `stop()` takes minutes, then the interpretation arrives with a warning | `interpretation.sse-timeout` | Engine's notice did not reach the page in time (the stream never connected, dropped, or Engine was slower than `analyzeTimeoutMs`), so the SDK read the stored one with `POST /analyze` | Check that your proxy forwards `/stream/{userProtocolId}` with no buffering and no total timeout | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/guides/timeouts.md |
| The session may have been interpreted twice | `analysis.regenerated` | `POST /analyze` answered `from_cache: false`: the stored interpretation was not there, so it generated one | Log it; do not retry `/analyze` in a loop | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/errors.md |
| The interpretation is refused | `E_TAXONOMY_REJECTED` | Today every `422` from `/analyze` arrives as this code: an id that is not the protocol's, one your organization cannot see, your organization not active in the interpretation service, frames without `featuresMeta` or a refused `voice`/`depth` | Check `featuresMeta` (own frame source) and the person's narrative first. Wrong id: recapture with the right one. Not visible: ask Neurologyca, then ask again without recapturing | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/errors.md |
| The interpretation is refused for the organization | `E_TENANT_UNKNOWN` | Not emitted with SDK 0.46.0 (that case arrives as `E_TAXONOMY_REJECTED`): the interpretation service has no active record of your organization | Ask Neurologyca, then ask for the interpretation again; do not recapture | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/errors.md |
| The analysis is refused for a missing axis | `E_AXES_MISSING` | Not emitted with SDK 0.46.0 (that case arrives as `E_TAXONOMY_REJECTED`): frames without `featuresMeta`, almost always from your own frame source | Fix the frames and ask for the interpretation again; do not recapture | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/errors.md |
| The interpretation arrives but is marked degraded | `analysis.degraded` | An enrichment step of the analysis failed; the narrative is built on partial inputs | Show it with a notice; the reason is in the payload's root `warnings` | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/errors.md |
| The interpretation is a placeholder | `E_ANALYSIS_FAILED` | The analysis came back as failed, with a `200` | Never show it as a result; the indicators are still valid | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/errors.md |
| `interpretation` is `null` | `protocol.not-interpreted` | Engine reports `interpreted: false` for the protocol (Measurement) | Expected: Measurement is never interpreted. Where Engine does not report `interpreted`, there is no warning: pass `triggerAnalysis: false` | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/guides/measurement.md |
| A step warns it was too short or too long | `step.duration-below-min` / `step.duration-above-max` | The step ended outside the catalogue's window | Close steps with `describeStep`'s `minSeconds` and `maxSeconds` in mind | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/steps.md |
| Ending a step throws | `E_STEP_DURATION` | With `stepDurations: 'enforce'`, an `end()` before the minimum or a `run({ durationMs })` out of range is refused (past the maximum, `end()` only warns; an ended `<video>` or stream source drops to warn) | Respect the window, or leave the default (warn) | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/steps.md |
| With webpack (Next.js) the build warns «Critical dependency: the request of a dependency is an expression» and the capture fails before the camera opens with «Cannot find module» | none | An older SDK brought a kodec whose `import()` of MediaPipe webpack replaced with an empty module (Vite and Rollup were not affected) | Update to the current SDK (0.46.0, which pins kodec 5.0.0); kodec is not a dependency you install yourself | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/start/install.md |
| A later step fails after the video or stream ends | `source.ended` | The source is over: the open step closes and no later step can be captured | Use a source long enough for the plan; on the warning, `abort()` or end your loop | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/on-device.md |
| Gaps in the series | `capture.throttled` | The tab was hidden or covered (the warning is relative to the session's own rate; read the device's rate with `onCodecEvent('capture-quality')`) | The gaps are real: keep the tab visible | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/on-device.md |
| With a capture token, sending pauses and the job takes longer | `transport.rate-limited` | Engine limited that capture's requests per minute (`429` `CAPTURE_TOKEN_RATE_LIMIT`); since 0.37.0 the SDK waits the `Retry-After` and sends the same request again | Nothing to fix: nothing is lost. Before 0.37.0 the capture failed instead | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/start/authentication.md |
| A 409 from Engine | `E_CONFLICT` | One of `SESSION_ABANDONED`, `POSITION_ALREADY_COMPLETED`, `MECHANIC_EXPIRED`, `MECHANIC_NOT_YET_AVAILABLE`, `MUST_START_AT_FIRST_POSITION`, `PROTOCOL_DEPRECATED`, `POSITION_RESERVED`, `CAPTURE_DISCARDED`, `CAPTURE_OUT_OF_ORDER`, `PROTOCOL_NOT_ANALYSED`, `CAPTURE_CANCELLED` | Branch on `reason`, with a default case, never on `expired` alone | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/errors.md |

## Your backend and the proxy

| Symptom | Code | Cause | Fix | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `npm install` answers 404 for `@neurologyca/*` | none | A scope line left over from the private previews sends `@neurologyca` to the old registry | Remove it and its `_authToken` from `.npmrc` and `~/.npmrc`, or pin `@neurologyca:registry=https://registry.npmjs.org/` in the project; never ask for a registry token, and never use the Engine key for npm | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/start/install.md |
| Every call answers 401 | `token.missing` | `baseUrl` points at Engine, not at your proxy, and there is no `getToken` | `engineBaseUrl: '/engine'` in `neuro.config.json` | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/backend.md |
| A 404 halfway through a capture | `E_ROUTE_NOT_ALLOWED` | A route the SDK calls is not in your proxy's list (a proxy written by hand without one of them) | Forward every route of the table, with its query | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/api/proxy.md |
| A live capture ends at once with `E_UPLOAD_FAILED` and the proxy answered 413 | `E_BODY_TOO_LARGE` (from `createEngineProxy`) or the platform's own 413 | The batch is over the proxy's body cap: a serverless function (4.5 MB on Vercel, 1 MB by default in a Next.js Pages Router API route, `pages/api`; an App Router route handler has no body parser), a low `maxBodyBytes`, or an SDK before 0.34.0, which cut batches only by count | A 4xx is final at the first try. Use the current SDK (0.46.0: at most 3 MB of frames per batch) and give the proxy a body cap of at least 4.5 MB; `createEngineProxy` in 0.21.0 reads up to 16 MiB by default | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/api/proxy.md |
| The proxy answers 503 | `E_RATE_LIMITED` | Engine rate-limited the token | Try again later; cache the token instead of minting one per request | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/backend.md |
| `admin.buildTenantKit(pid)` fails for Measurement (and the enrolment made before it is left without a capture) | `E_KIT_NO_CONTEXT` | An older `capture-server` only looks at the interpretation context of the protocol's group, and Measurement has none; 0.21.0 reads `interpreted` from Engine's catalogue, so it still throws only against a catalogue that does not publish it | Use `capture-server` 0.21.0 and branch on `kit.kind` (`'measurement'`). Otherwise use a fixed configuration, the one the console's Measurement kit carries (https://devlab.neurologyca.com/integration, its `neuro.config.json`: `engineBaseUrl: '/engine'`, `taxonomy: { contextId: 'measurement' }`, and `triggerAnalysis: false` for your capture). Resolve the kit before `admin.enrol` | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/guides/measurement.md, https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/backend.md |
| The proxy refuses with 403 | `E_FORBIDDEN` | Your `authorize` said no, or the origin is not allowed | Check your session and `ownsAll` | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/backend.md |

## Deferred (server-side) · Preview

| Symptom | Code | Cause | Fix | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The recording does not start | `E_RECORDER_UNSUPPORTED` | The browser cannot record MP4/H.264 (Firefox) | Another browser; Firefox is not a target | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/deferred.md |
| `uploadRecording` rejects | `E_UPLOAD_NETWORK`, `E_UPLOAD_NOT_NOTIFIED`, `E_UPLOAD_AUTH`, `E_UPLOAD_REJECTED`, `E_UPLOAD_CONFLICT`, `E_UPLOAD_TOO_LARGE`, `E_UPLOAD_LOCAL_UNREADABLE`, `E_UPLOAD_PROTOCOL`, `E_UPLOAD_ABORTED` | See `code` and `retryable` | Retryable: call again later, the local copy is kept. Not retryable: see the table | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/deferred.md |
| `submit` is refused | `E_QUEUE_FULL` | Too many unfinished jobs for your client of the service | Retry `submit` later with a growing wait: `retryAfterMs` is empty | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/processing.md |
| `submit` or `getJob` throw while the job waits | `E_PROCESSOR_PROTOCOL` | A `capture-server` older than 0.11.0 does not know `interpreting` | Upgrade `capture-server` before the service that waits | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/processing.md |
| The job ends `succeeded` with `interpretation: 'timeout'` and the video is never deleted | `interpretation: 'timeout'` | Your `onEvent` closed the job at `interpreting`, so the token URL denied the renewal | Branch on `interpreting` first: delete the video, keep the job open | [deferred.md](deferred.md) |
| The camera stops or the tab closes while recording | `E_RECORDING_LOST` | Nothing was kept | `abort()` to release the reservation, then record again | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/deferred.md |

## Transcription

| Symptom | Code | Cause | Fix | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The transcript comes back in English | `stt.autodetect` | The language was autodetected | Pin `transcribe: { language }` | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/guides/csp-transcription.md |
| Voice steps reach Engine with no text, very little, or translated | none, or `stt.silent` when nothing is left | `transcribe.language` is not the language spoken: it is forced, never detected; the model translates into it (nothing warns) or kodec discards what comes out | Declare the person's language per capture (from your backend, or ask on the consent screen); check `SessionReport.transcription` (`vad.utterances`, `emitted`, `discarded*`) | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/guides/csp-transcription.md |
| A speech step will not be transcribed | `transcription.off-on-voice-step` / `transcribeSteps.excludes-voice-step` | A step with `expectsSpeech: 'yes'` runs with `transcribe` off, or `transcribeSteps` leaves it out | Turn `transcribe` on and include that step's type in `transcribeSteps` (or leave `transcribeSteps` out: absent means every step) | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/on-device.md |
| No step is transcribed | `transcribeSteps.noMatch` | No type in `transcribeSteps` exists in the plan | Take the types from `PlanStep.type` of the plan, never guess them | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/errors.md |
| The microphone worked and nothing was transcribed | `stt.silent` | Utterances discarded for low confidence, repetition loops or the Silero gate (minifying no longer breaks it) | Read the report's `transcription.discarded*` counters; with all of them at zero, the transcription and bundlers guide says what else to read | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/guides/csp-transcription.md |
| The frame rate collapses while transcribing | `stt.mainThread` | Speech runs on the main thread because its worker did not start: a relative `transcribe.selfHost` URL, `transcribe.transformers` without `transformersUrl`, or a blocked worker | Read `workerFailedBecause` and `workerBlockedByCsp` in the report, and `SessionReport.captureQuality` | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/guides/csp-transcription.md |
| Transcription stopped in one browser although the video runs well | `stt` with `blocker: 'video-cost-veto'` | An earlier capture ran at 8 fps or less (typically a hidden tab) and kodec stored a provisional veto in that browser | Use SDK 0.41.0 or later: the first capture that shows video above 8 fps deletes it (`SessionReport.transcriptionSetup.provisionalVetoLifted`). Keep the tab visible; to retry by hand, behind a button the person presses (never automatically), `readTranscriptionVeto(kodec)` and, if there is a veto, `forgetTranscriptionVeto(kodec)`, with the `kodec` that `@neurologyca/capture` exports (never kodec's `forgetMemoryLimit`). The veto belongs to that browser, not to the person, and comes back if the video slows down again | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/guides/csp-transcription.md |
| A voice step has no `transcript` in `/results` and nothing on the page says why | `stt` with a `blocker`, or none | Your page shows only the last warning and a later one (such as `source.ended`) replaced `stt`; or the SDK heard no speech in that step | Keep the `stt` warning in its own slot and show it visibly; read `onReport` when the capture ends (also with `endedBy: 'abort'` or `'error'`; `CaptureOutcome` does not carry it): `transcription.stepsTranscription[]` with `transcribed: false` or `emitted: 0` heard no speech, and `transcriptionSetup` gives the model and language | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/guides/csp-transcription.md |
| The device cannot transcribe | `no-webgpu`, `ladder-exhausted`, `video-cost-veto`, `no-memory-to-learn`, `dtype-required` | The device lacks what the speech model needs | The indicators go on; transcription is skipped on that device | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/guides/csp-transcription.md |
| Speech stops after an SDK upgrade, with ONNX Runtime self-hosted | `stt` (`subStage: 'stt-runtime'`, `reason: 'network'`, `cut.code: 'stt-runtime-missing'`) | A `wasmPaths` string is now a prefix that must end in `/` and serve the executor's ONNX Runtime pair (the Asyncify pair alone fails) | End it in `/` and serve both pairs, or pass `wasmPaths` as `{ wasm, mjs }` | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/guides/csp-transcription.md |
| Works locally, not in production | none | The production CSP blocks what the SDK loads (MediaPipe, the speech models, the `blob:` worker) | Copy the CSP of the guide, or host the files yourself | https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/guides/csp-transcription.md |
